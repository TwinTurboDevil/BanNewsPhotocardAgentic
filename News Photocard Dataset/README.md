# BanglaFakeCard — 1,000-photocard subset

This directory contains the 1,000-photocard subset of the
BanglaFakeCard benchmark used in the paper "Evidence-Grounded
Agentic Multimodal Verification of Bangla News Photocards".

## Composition

- **Total photocards:** 1,000
- **Real class:** 500 photocards, drawn from 10 Bangladeshi news outlets
- **Fake class:** 500 photocards, including parody-outlet impersonations
- **Outlets represented:** 14
- **News genres:** 8 (politics, international, sports, crime,
  entertainment, business, health, disaster)
